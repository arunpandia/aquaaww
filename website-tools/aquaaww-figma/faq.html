Questions

Is AquaAww free?

_

Yes. It is free, has no ads and needs no account. The code is open source on GitHub.

What does it actually do?

You upload a short video of someone you love. At the interval you choose, that video pops up on your screen, floating over whatever you are doing, with two buttons: I drank, or remind me later.

Do my videos leave my device?

No. Videos, settings and your daily count stay on your phone or computer. AquaAww has no account system and no tracking inside the app.
