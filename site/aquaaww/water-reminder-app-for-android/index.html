Water reminder app for Android

AquaAww turns the usual water-reminder beep into a short video of someone you love. On Android it floats over whatever you are doing, so a reminder in the middle of Instagram or YouTube is hard to miss, and it also sends a notification with the same two buttons.

Download free for Android

What you need

  • Android 5.1 or newer
  • About 20 MB of space
  • Notifications allowed (the app asks on first launch)
  • Optional: permission to display over other apps for the floating buddy

How to install on Android

  1. Fill in your name and email on the AquaAww page and tap Android. The file AquaAww.apk downloads.
  2. Open the file. If Android blocks it, allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps, then open the file again.
  3. Open AquaAww. Allow notifications when asked, then allow display over other apps if you want the floating buddy.
  4. Upload a short video, choose how often and which hours, and you are done.

Tips

  • Keep the video under 30 MB and about 3 to 6 seconds long.
  • If reminders stop on some phones, set battery use for AquaAww to Unrestricted in Android settings.
  • Use Test reminder in the app to see exactly what you will get.
